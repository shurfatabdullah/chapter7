# Chapter 7 References: Emerging Challenges in Modern Power Systems for Inter-Area Oscillation Studies

This folder contains the Chapter 7 draft and the supporting references used in the chapter.

## Files

- `Chapter7_Draft.md`: Current draft of Chapter 7.
- `C1_High_PV_Penetration_Eastern_Interconnection.pdf`: High photovoltaic penetration and its impact on inter-area oscillations in the U.S. Eastern Interconnection.
- `C2_High_Wind_Eastern_Interconnection.pdf`: High wind penetration and inter-area oscillation damping in the U.S. Eastern Interconnection.
- `C3_Inter_Area_Modes_and_Inertia.pdf`: Relationship between inter-area modes and power system inertia.
- `C4_Inertia_Distribution_EIR_Location.pdf`: Inertia distribution, center of inertia, and location of electronically interfaced resources.
- `C5_IBR_Location_and_Parameters.pdf`: Impact of inverter-based resource location and parameters on inter-area oscillation modes.
- `C6_VSG_Inter_Area_Oscillation.pdf`: Inter-area oscillation analysis of power systems integrated with virtual synchronous generators.
- `C7_GFM_Wind_Turbine_Interaction.pdf`: Grid-forming wind turbine interaction with inter-area oscillations and model-order reduction.
- `C8_Utility_Scale_PV_Damping.pdf`: Utility-scale photovoltaic plant damping support using local phasor measurements.
- `C9_DFIG_Wind_POD_Damping.pdf`: DFIG-based wind plant damping support using active/reactive POD control.
- `C10_Forced_Oscillation_Converter_Resources.pdf`: Forced oscillations in power systems with converter-controlled resources.
- `C11_Oscillation_Localization_Survey.pdf`: Oscillation localization techniques in power systems.

## Review Instruction

Review the chapter strictly against references C1–C11. Any unsupported, overstated, or weakly cited claim should be identified and revised. Keep the citation labels in the format [C1]–[C11].
